Being self-employed (autónomo) in Spain means filing several forms with the tax agency (AEAT) throughout the year, some quarterly and some annual. Here's a rundown of the most common ones and when they're due.

## Modelo 130: income tax installment payment

Filed by autónomos under direct estimation (normal or simplified). It's an advance payment toward your annual income tax return, calculated on your income and deductible expenses for the quarter.

- **Frequency:** quarterly.
- **Deadlines:** April 1–20, July 1–20, October 1–20, and January 1–30 (fourth quarter).

If more than 70 % of your income already has IRPF withholding applied (for example, invoices to businesses), you may be exempt from filing it.

## Modelo 303: VAT self-assessment

Declares the VAT charged on your invoices and the VAT paid on your expenses. The difference is what you pay (or get refunded) each quarter.

- **Frequency:** quarterly.
- **Deadlines:** same as Modelo 130.

## Modelo 111: withholdings and payments on account

Filed by autónomos who act as withholding agents, meaning they've applied IRPF withholding to other professionals or employees (for example, if you have staff or subcontract other autónomos with withholding).

- **Frequency:** quarterly.
- **Deadlines:** same as Modelo 130.

## Modelo 349: intra-community transactions

Mandatory if you invoice clients or receive invoices from suppliers in other European Union countries (intra-community transactions).

- **Frequency:** monthly or quarterly, depending on transaction volume.

## Modelo 390: annual VAT summary

A summary of all the VAT declared during the year across the quarterly Modelo 303 filings. It doesn't involve an additional payment, it's informational.

- **Frequency:** annual.
- **Deadline:** January 1–30.

## Modelo 190: annual withholdings summary

The annual equivalent of Modelo 111: it gathers a summary of all withholdings and payments on account made during the year.

- **Frequency:** annual.
- **Deadline:** January 1–31.

## Modelo 100: income tax return (IRPF)

The annual income tax return, where what you paid on account during the year (Modelo 130 and withholdings) is reconciled against the final result based on your total income.

- **Frequency:** annual.
- **Deadline:** April through late June.

## In summary

- Quarterly: Modelo 130 (income tax), Modelo 303 (VAT) and, if applicable, Modelo 111 (withholdings) and Modelo 349 (intra-community transactions).
- Annual: Modelo 390 (VAT summary), Modelo 190 (withholdings summary) and Modelo 100 (income tax return).
- Not every autónomo files every form: it depends on your tax regime, whether you apply withholdings, and whether you deal with EU clients or suppliers.
